Iron Rivals Bingo
User guide
Setup order (important)
You must do Auto-locate Chat before locking IGN.
  1. Auto-locate Chat (Setup panel) → locks chatbox.
  2. Enter IGN and lock it.
  3. Select Bingo + Team and lock setup (plugin refreshes).
Barrows chest setup
  1. Open the Barrows chest.
  2. First time only: hover near the top bar / close button and press Alt+1 to locate it.
  3. After that, opening the chest will auto-scan and submit Barrows uniques.
  4. If needed: press Alt+1 while the chest is open to force an immediate scan.
Where to press Alt+1: hover your mouse over the chest's top-right close button (red X) or anywhere along the top bar.

Tip: If the chest is not detected, close and re-open it, then press Alt+1 again near the top bar.
