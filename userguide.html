Iron Rivals Bingo
User guide
Setup order (important)
You must do Auto-locate Chat before locking IGN.
  1. Auto-locate Chat (Setup panel) → locks chatbox.
  2. Enter IGN and lock it.
  3. Select Bingo + Team and lock setup (plugin refreshes).
Barrows chest setup
  1. Open the Barrows chest.
  2. First time: hover near the chest top bar and press Alt+1 to locate it.
  3. After that, opening the chest will auto-scan and submit Barrows uniques.
  4. If needed: press Alt+1 while the chest is open to force an immediate scan.
Tip: if the chest is not detected, re-open it and press Alt+1 again near the top bar to re-locate.
Troubleshooting
  • Nothing submits: confirm Chat locked → IGN locked → Bingo/Team locked.
  • Chat not found: make sure chat is visible (not minimized), then use Auto-locate Chat again.
  • Chest not detected: open chest and press Alt+1 near the top bar.
